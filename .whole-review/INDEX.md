# Index of files read (summary per file; full content in notes/<path>.md)

- package.json: The project's manifest: an ES-module private package with the Vite/Vitest/TypeScript scripts, the dev tools and the runtime libraries (three and its BVH/CSG/path tracer add-ons, pdfjs-dist, polyclip-ts).
- tsconfig.json: TypeScript compiler options for src and tests: ES2022 target, bundler module resolution (Vite), the strict family plus noUncheckedIndexedAccess and exactOptionalPropertyTypes, unused locals/params as errors, isolatedModules, globals only from vitest and node. It is what `npx tsc --noEmit` (the review's static check) checks.
- vitest.config.ts: Vitest configuration: the default excludes plus other sessions' worktrees (.claude/**) and Stryker's sandbox (.stryker-tmp/**).
- index.html: The page Vite serves and builds from: charset, viewport, title, favicon from public/, the Inter font (400/500/600) from Google Fonts with preconnects, the #app root and the module entry src/app/main.ts.
- src/app/actions.ts: The list of every named action (tools, views, edit, file, settings, levels, help): id, translated label and group, search keywords in both languages, its key (own or remapped by the user), enabled state and what it runs. The palette, the ribbon and the File menu read this list; keys are resolved through app/keymap.ts.
- src/app/app-files.ts: The project as a file: rename (one undo step), save as JSON (download), the download helper, the file base name, a new project, and opening a JSON file (into the browser's project list when there is a database, else in place as one undo step).
- src/app/app.ts: The application object: the whole interface state (UiState, ~45 fields), the store wrapped so every command also settles locked dimensions and, when walls change, keeps openings and rooms (keepOpenings/keepRooms); the derived geometry and refresh/render; and ~200 methods, most of them one-line delegations to the app/*-ui.ts modules, frame.ts and chrome.ts. Holds the renderers (plan, 3D, overlay, cube), the clipboard, the Modify tool's snapshots, the keymap and ribbon state.
- src/app/boot.ts: Chooses the project the app opens with: the last one open in the browser's IndexedDB (or the next readable one, saying when the last one could not be read); the first time, the old local-storage document becomes the first project; without a database, local storage only.
- src/app/browser-data.ts: Pure helper for the project browser: how many walls use each wall type, on one level or on all.
- src/app/carry.ts: Rooms and their walls: which walls close a room in, which rooms go along with a move or turn of all their walls, moving room points, a per-document geometry cache, and keepRooms: after any wall change, a room that was on its own and lost that gets a new point in what is left of its space.
- src/app/chrome.ts: Renders the interface around the views on every render: the browser drawer (cached by a key), the top bar, the ribbon (cached by a key, from the action list plus the context entries), the 3D and plan view bars, the level list, the footer's hint, key hints and measure field, the tool's Finish button, and places the floating bar over the selection (plan or 3D) clear of the panel, handles, dimension values and labels.
- src/app/collision.ts: Pure collision helpers for moving and placing loose elements: convex hulls, a separating-axis overlap test, shapes of moving kinds and of obstacles (wall blocks and solid kinds) on a level, a move clamped in 2 cm steps with sliding, and the nearest free place for a placement.
- src/app/context-actions.ts: Which context actions (ids) the right-click menu and the floating bar offer for a selection: by what is selected (nothing, several, a guide, a wall, a door, a window, a room, anything else), Move first when the Modify tools can take it, six at most.
- src/app/context-menu.ts: Turns the context ids chosen by context-actions.ts into runnable entries (label, icon, key, run) for the right-click menu, the floating bar and the ribbon's contextual tab. Its LOOK table holds each id's icon and a literal key; its run table wires each id to an App method or a command: a second definition of options the action list (actions.ts) already defines.
- src/app/dim-labels.ts: Pure placement of temporary dimension value labels on screen: each at its line's middle, or slid along its line in 4 px steps, or stepped off it vertically, until clear of the labels placed before it.
- src/app/document.ts: The project document type (Doc v67) and its levels and walls; the fields kept for the areas taken out (KEPT_FIELDS, Doc.kept); a new document's defaults; and the derived helpers every module uses: level lookup, start level, wall numbers, a wall's top/base (own or its type's), its heights (level, fixed, to a level, under a roof), roof covers per level (cached), a wall's band, its thickness, and the drawing settings.
- src/app/frame.ts: Draws one frame on every render: the plan with its whole overlay (or the 3D scene and its overlay), the temporary dimension value buttons/fields, the browser drawer and chrome, and the properties panel (rebuilt only when its key changes) with every panel action wired to commands; also the temporary dimensions of the selection and their edit fields.
- src/app/geometry.ts: The derivation of everything drawn from the document: walls joined by height slices per level (plan pieces, 3D blocks under roofs, seams, materials), openings placed and cut (with joint and swing-clash warnings), rooms from the uncut walls and separators, spaces, and the registry kinds; plus hit tests (opening, wall), bounds, temporary hide/isolate and hidden categories.
- src/app/guide-ui.ts: The guide tool and guides set off a wall face or another guide: modes (line, point, circle), clicks, a circle's typed radius, the guides shown in plan for snapping, the guide draft (start, move, offset typed or pointed, place, cancel), and clearing the level's guides.
- src/app/handles.ts: Pure positions and hit test of the selection's handles on screen: end squares and stretch arrows of one wall, the bend diamond of one arc wall, the turn handle under the selection (clear of the other handles and of the temporary dimension values), and a door's two flip controls; used in plan and (since 2c739a7) in 3D.
- src/app/input-buttons.ts: Wires every fixed button of the shell to the app: top bar (undo, redo, projects, views, level list, language, theme, save, open, rename, panel), File menu (from FILE_MENU and the action list), ribbon wheel scrolling, footer toggles, the view bar (levels shown, explode, cut, x-ray, box, ortho, style, quality, scale, visibility, show all, light sliders), temporary view reset, shortcuts and commands.
- src/app/input-keys.ts: The drawing's keyboard: one window keydown listener that skips fields and dialogs, then tries Tab (next snap), the user's remapped keys (keymap), Ctrl shortcuts, view keys, Alt guides, digits for a guide, arrow locks, nudges, Escape cancels, typing into the measure field, Escape per tool, Enter per tool, Backspace/Delete, Home, ?, and every tool's own key as a literal check.
- src/app/input.ts: Wires all pointer and keyboard input in one closure: the dialogs' openers and the action list, the context menu, tracker and marquee, world points (plan, 3D floor, 3D surfaces), snapping inputs for every tool, picking, then per-tool behaviour for pointer move, press, right click and release as long if-chains, the measure field's keys, Alt guides, arrow/Shift locks, Tab snaps, project rename and the shortcuts help.
- src/app/keep-in-view.ts: Pure helpers that keep the selection seen in the plan: the free part of the view (not covered by panel, sheet or drawer, less margins and room above for the float bar), a zoom-out factor to fit a box in it, and the smallest pan that brings a box into it.
- src/app/keep-openings.ts: Pure follow-up run after every wall change (store wrapper and previews): a door or window placed by a fromStart/fromEnd rule, measured as a gap from the face of another wall beside it (wall-stops.ts), gets its stored distance rewritten so that gap stays the same when that face (or the host's end) moved.
- src/app/keyboard-select.ts: Pure step through an ordered list of element ids from the current selection, forward or back, wrapping round; used by N / Shift+N.
- src/app/keymap.ts: The user's remapped shortcuts: a map action id → key (or null for none), how keys are written and normalised, the key an event is, an action's current key, what a key does under the map (run an action, or blocked), and assigning a key (taking it from any other action).
- src/app/kind-tools.ts: The click handlers of the tools that place the registry's kinds (guardrail, stair, roof, beam, separator, curtain wall, object, column, grid axis, section, callout, dimension, measure marks) and the level mark/note, plus their drafts shown in plan and the elevations (around the model and inside rooms). Functions over the App (app.ts delegates to them).
- src/app/layer-sets.ts: Layer combinations (named sets of hidden categories) and graphic overrides (wall types and room uses filled in a colour of their own in plan), their guards, the commands that save/delete/replace them, the colour maps the plan reads, and the walls' renovation phases.
- src/app/library-link.ts: Libraries (object definitions and materials) shared by link: copying a library's link to the clipboard (refused past 100 kB), and opening a link from the address (at start or on hashchange) as one undo step, the hash taken off the address first.
- src/app/main.ts: The entry module (index.html's script): the stylesheet, then boot() picks the project, then the App is built on it, input and tooltips wired, and library links opened.
- src/app/model-import.ts: Bringing a glTF (.glb, or .gltf with embedded buffers) or OBJ model into the project as a library object: loaded by three.js's loaders (imported on demand), its meshes turned to plan axes and grouped by material colour, made a definition (io/import-mesh.ts), added in one undo step, then placed with the object tool.
- src/app/modify-ui.ts: The Modify tools over the selection (move, copy, rotate, mirror, scale, offset) driven by tools/modify-tool.ts's state machine, the copy series ("3x", "/3"), and the wall tools split, corner and merge; plus the marks a Modify tool shows. Functions over the App (app.ts delegates).
- src/app/next-element.ts: The "next element" a placing tool makes (the template the goal names as the model for root cause 1): one per kind slot (columns, beams, stairs, railings, roofs, floors, ceilings, curtains, objects, rooms), as the user changed it, else the last of its kind, else the kind's defaults; the element a click makes from it; and its fields, taken from the kind's own panel fields minus a list of ids that only a placed element has. Walls, doors and windows are NOT here (their next lives in WallDefaults / OpeningSize and the separate nextWallFields / nextOpeningFields).
- src/app/object-handles.ts: Drag handles of one selected parametric object (its definition's hotspots) and of one selected door or window (jambs → width, head → height, a window's sill), in plan and 3D: where they are on screen and their axis; picking one; the drag (pointer motion along the screen axis, divided by how fast the handle moves with its value, so it stays under the pointer); one undo step on release; and drawing them.
- src/app/opening-ui.ts: What the door and window tool reads from the screen: the wall under the cursor as an Aim (station, side, other openings, the stops of walls meeting it), the same along a given wall for the keyboard, the rule in words, the temporary dimension of the opening being placed, and the straight wall under a 3D ray (aimed at the wall's middle plane, not its cut mesh).
- src/app/persist.ts: Saving in the browser: the interface preferences (localStorage), the old single document (localStorage) and the saving of the open project (to its IndexedDB record through io/project-db.ts when one is set, else localStorage): the latest version only, when the browser is idle, and at once when the page is hidden or closed.
- src/app/projects.ts: The projects kept in the browser's database: new, open, a file opened as a new project, list, duplicate, rename and delete. Switching saves the project left first and resets the store (its undo history left behind).
- src/app/reference-ui.ts: Reference drawings under the current level's plan: a picture, a PDF's first page (rendered with pdf.js at 150 dpi to a PNG) or a DXF's lines, added as one command and selected; and calibrating one by two points and the real distance typed in a dialog.
- src/app/ribbon-tabs.ts: The ribbon's content by tab (Home, Architecture, Structure, Annotate, View, Manage), the contextual Modify | <selection> tab built from the context menu's entries, the Place <element> tab with the next element's fields (walls and openings from their separate next lists, others from next-element.ts) and the tool's ways, the ribbon's objects, and the File menu.
- src/app/room-tag.ts: The text of a room's tag in plan by the drawing's choice: number and name (default), name, number, or number and name with the ceiling height; its area line except for the number alone; and the short form (the number) for narrow rooms.
- src/app/room-ui.ts: The room tool (a click inside closed walls makes a room, numbered on the level), the floor tool (a click in a room floors it or makes its ceiling; otherwise an outline of corners), holes in a floor, a room's floor and ceiling, and finishing an outline. Functions over the App.
- src/app/saved-views.ts: Saved views in the document: a 3D view (camera position/target, its View3D settings) or a plan view (level, zoom, centre, drawing scale), with what they show (hidden categories, overrides); the commands that save, delete, rename/caption and reorder them; the next free name; and the file reader's guard.
- src/app/section-box.ts: Pure geometry of the 3D section box (six faces dragged by handles) and of the vertical section line: faces, normals, moving a face (never thinner than 10 cm), the first box around the model, its edges, a screen drag turned into metres, stored-value readers, and the caps (the filled cut surfaces) where the box's faces or the section's plane go through upright prisms (wall blocks, floors), following a sloped top.
- src/app/section-drawing.ts: A section or elevation drawn from the model: every solid as triangles (wall blocks by material, opening parts, kinds' meshes), clipped to the mark's plane, ends and depth; the cut filled (wall layers by material with hatch) and outlined; what lies beyond as paper-filled faces far to near with their feature edges; levels, rooms crossed and cut openings; and the painting on paper with dimensions.
- src/app/section-view.ts: A section or elevation drawn for the sections dialog: the model's triangles and rooms cached per document, each mark's drawing cached per model, and the drawing painted on a white canvas of a frame's size at a dpi with the project's pens at its scale and the light theme's colours.
- src/app/select-drag.ts: The select tool's gestures: press, drag and release over walls, rooms, registry elements, a single door or window (slid along or onto another wall), a dimension line, wall grips/arrows/rotate/arc handles, and the window/crossing marquee; each drag one command on release, Esc cancels; plus marquee picking and a convex hull.
- src/app/select-similar.ts: Select similar (Revit's Select All Instances): the selection plus every element on the level shown that the registry's kinds call alike (similarKinds).
- src/app/selection-ui.ts: Selection actions over the App: all walls of the level, stepping to the next element (N), nudging by the arrows, clearing, temporary hide/isolate/reset, copy and paste to another level (rooms renumbered on), delete, select similar (only what is shown), and walls of a type.
- src/app/stacked.ts: Pure check for walls standing on a wall of the level just below, parallel and overlapping, whose exterior faces do not line up (a step on the façade), with the shift that aligns them (Chief Architect's Align With Wall Below).
- src/app/temp-dims.ts: Revit-like temporary dimensions of the one selected element: a straight wall's length (on its quieter side) and the clear gaps to the nearest parallel facing walls; an arc wall's chord and radius; a door/window's distances to the nearest wall stop on each side (or to the opening it is measured from). Each carries the command a typed value makes.
- src/app/view-3d.ts: The 3D view's interface state (kept in preferences, not the document): which levels show, explode, cut, x-ray, section box, orthographic, section line, visual style, quality, brightness and sun; the levels shown, whether exploding, a level's lift, the cut height, and reading a stored value with defaults.
- src/app/view-keys.ts: Pure mapping of view keys: + or = zoom in, − zoom out (not while a measure is being typed), Shift+arrows pan 80 px; Ctrl/Alt combinations are left to other shortcuts.
- src/app/view-ui.ts: The views over the App: the section box (on/off, handles, drag direction, set), explode, the 3D view's options, plan/3D switch and first framing, keeping the plan's zoom, sliding the plan or 3D view so the selection is not under a panel, what the panels cover, frame all/selection, the four elevations, a vertical section at a guide, and the view keys.
- src/app/views-ui.ts: Saved views over the App: saving the current 3D camera and options or the plan's level/zoom/position/drawing scale (with the hidden categories and overrides) under the next free name; going back to one (level, what it shows, camera or plan framing, its drawing scale); deleting one.
- src/app/wall-stops.ts: The stops of a wall: the stations along its reference line where the faces of the other straight walls of its level that touch or cross it meet that line; the nearest stop before/after a station. Doors and windows are measured from these (the face of the wall beside them), which is what ties an opening to a neighbouring wall (root cause 2).
- src/app/wall-ui.ts: The wall tool over the App (preview of the wall, rectangle or arc being drawn; wall number tags; measures shown while stretching, bending or turning; the typed end, rectangle corner; orientation and Space flip; making a wall from the defaults; the tool's events; the clockwise-loop warning) and the footer field's typed values for every tool (series of copies, Modify values, guides, arc radius, rectangle sides, points, lengths and angles, an opening's distance, a selected wall's length).
- src/assets.d.ts: An ambient module declaration so TypeScript accepts Vite's "?url" imports (the pdf.js worker's address in app/reference-ui.ts) as a default string export.
- src/core/collisions.ts: A module-level flag mirroring the app's "collisions on" setting, so field definitions (an object's elevation minimum) can read it without the App.
- src/core/guards.ts: Shared type guards for data read from files: a non-null object, a finite number, a point with finite x and y.
- src/core/id.ts: New element ids: the kind's prefix, an underscore and the first 8 hex digits of crypto.randomUUID().
- src/core/measure.ts: The typed-measure parser (never eval): lengths with units (mm, cm, m, ft/', in/", feet-and-inches), plain numbers, "x;y" and "@dx;dy" points, "length<angle"; + - * / x × and parentheses; numbers with comma or point; a bare number in the project's length unit; the project's named lengths (variables).
- src/core/rtree.ts: A static R-tree packed once by sort-tile-recursive (16 entries a node) and queried for the boxes a box touches; plus the box around a set of points.
- src/core/store.ts: The document store: commands turn one immutable version into the next, each dispatch one undo step (unchanged result: no step); undo/redo stacks with labels; reset (new history); listeners told after every change.
- src/core/tolerance.ts: The geometric tolerances (meant as their single source): wall ends meeting (1 mm), in-line angle (0.6°), miter limit (10×), minimum wall length (1 mm), minimum element size (1 cm), smallest area kept (1e-9 m²), arc sag (0.5 mm).
- src/core/vector.ts: 2D vector helpers: Point2, vec, add, sub, scale, dot, cross, length, distance, left perpendicular, normalize (zero stays zero) and the intersection of two parametric lines.
- src/elements/beam/commands.ts: Beam commands: add a beam; update the section/top/end offsets/shape/plate of several beams (refusing non-positive sizes and non-finite offsets; no step when nothing changes).
- src/elements/beam/fields.ts: The beam's panel fields: length (readout, "—" when the selected beams differ), width, depth, shape (rectangle or I), I plate thickness (only when all are I), top offset and end offset (a sloping beam).
- src/elements/beam/schema.ts: The beam element: a bar between two points of a level hanging from the level's top (with offsets at start and end), its width and depth, a rectangle or I section; defaults; its section polygon, id check and plan outline.
- src/elements/column/commands.ts: Column commands: add a column; update section, angle, top offset, shape, plate and the level its top reaches (null back to its own level's top) of several columns, refusing non-positive sizes and non-finite values, no step when nothing changes.
- src/elements/column/fields.ts: The column's panel fields: section shape, width, depth, angle in degrees, plate (profiles only), the level its top reaches (its own top or a level above) and the top offset.
- src/elements/column/schema.ts: The column element: a section at a point of a level, turned by an angle, from the level's floor up to its top (or another level's floor) plus an offset; shapes rectangle, round, I, H, L with plates; defaults; id check and its plan outline per shape.
- src/elements/contract.ts: The element contract: what each kind of element tells the app (prefix, category, level, drawing layer, panel title, validator, derivation and its memo, holders, Modify-tool behaviour, plan painting, 3D solid, caps for sections, outlines/hit/pick for selection, moved/mapped/copied, select-similar, fields), with the helper interfaces the plan, the panel and the Modify tools lend it; plus DXF/GLB export members left from the exports taken out.
- src/elements/curtain/commands.ts: Curtain wall commands: add one; update base offset, height, vertical/horizontal grid rules, mullion size, depth and panel kind of several (refusing non-positive or non-finite values and invalid grid rules; no step when nothing changes).
- src/elements/curtain/fields.ts: The curtain wall's panel fields: length (readout), height, base offset, each direction's division (mode spacing/count/max with its spacing or panel count), mullion, depth, panel kind, and the cells count.
- src/elements/curtain/geometry.ts: A curtain wall as built (pure): outline in plan, vertical and horizontal grid lines, mullions over the full height at each vertical line, transoms between mullions at each horizontal line, and a pane or opaque panel in each cell, all as plan rectangles between heights.
- src/elements/curtain/schema.ts: The curtain wall element (a line on a level, foot offset, optional height, vertical and horizontal grid rules, mullion size and depth, panel kind), its defaults, id check, grid-rule guard and where a rule's lines fall.
- src/elements/dimension/anchor.ts: A dimension's ends (pure): what holds a point put on the plan (an opening's jamb, a wall from its nearer end, or a free point), where a held point is now, a dimension placed with its line and length, the offset through a point, and a point's distance to a segment.
- src/elements/dimension/auto.ts: Automatic dimensioning (pure): for each straight wall of the level (or the walls given), on its outer face (no room behind), a chain from corner to corner through every door and window jamb and the wall's overall length past it, each end held by what it measures, stacked past dimensions already on that side.
- src/elements/dimension/commands.ts: Permanent dimension commands: add one; set a line's offset (drag); set several lines' distance keeping each side; add many (auto dimensions) as one step; lock at the current length or unlock; make chains equal (EQ) or not.
- src/elements/dimension/constraints.ts: Keeping locked and equal (EQ) dimensions after every model change (pure): a locked dimension whose length changed moves what holds its other end (a free point, a wall moved or stretched, a door/window along its wall) by the difference; an EQ chain moves its middle points to equal steps; repeated until nothing moves (12 rounds), or the change refused naming a dimension.
- src/elements/dimension/fields.ts: The permanent dimension's panel fields: its length (or "lost" when an end is gone, "varies" for several), its line's distance off what it measures, Lock/Unlock and Equal/Unequal actions, and a note when a lock or EQ holds the model.
- src/elements/dimension/schema.ts: The permanent dimension: two anchored ends (on a wall from an end with along/offset, on an opening's jamb with offset, or a free point), the line's offset, an optional locked length, chain id and EQ flag; id check; the element an end is held by.
- src/elements/drawing/schema.ts: The drawing settings: the plan's scale (1:20 to 1:500), the pen widths by role at 1:100, what room tags say; the pen scale factor by scale, the detail level by scale, text sizes on paper, paper sizes, and the file guard.
- src/elements/grid/carry.ts: What goes with a selected grid axis when it moves: the walls lying on it (schema.ts onGrid) and the registry's elements standing on its line (columns' centres, beams), on every level.
- src/elements/grid/commands.ts: Grid axis commands: add an axis; rename one (trimmed, upper-cased; an empty name or one another axis has is refused, silently).
- src/elements/grid/fields.ts: A grid axis's panel fields: its name (only when one axis is selected) and its length (readout, "—" when several differ).
- src/elements/grid/schema.ts: The grid axis element (name, start, end; on every level), id check, the next letters (A → B, Z → AA), the next axis's name (numbers up and down the plan, letters across) and the walls lying on an axis.
- src/elements/guide/commands.ts: Guide commands: add a guide; clear every guide of a level (no step when there are none).
- src/elements/guide/fields.ts: The panel for selected guides: a note saying what a guide is, and an action clearing the level's guides.
- src/elements/guide/schema.ts: The guide element: an endless construction line (a point and a unit direction) on a level, or a point or a circle (radius); snapping uses them; saved and undone like the rest; line-guide and id checks.
- src/elements/kinds/beam.ts: Beams in the registry: the solid derived (hanging from the level's top with start/end offsets), the 3D sweep of its section along its axis, validation, selection, moving/mapping, plan drawing (dashed, above the cut), fields; plus the dead DXF/export members (F-0047).
- src/elements/kinds/column.ts: Columns in the registry: validation, derivation (floor to the level's top or another level's floor, plus offset, at least 1 cm tall), selection, moving and mapping (turned by the transform), plan drawing (cut, structure fill), 3D extrusion of the outline, fields; plus dead DXF/export members (F-0047).
- src/elements/kinds/curtain.ts: Curtain walls in the registry: validation, derivation (frame and panels with their materials), selection, moving/mapping, plan drawing (two thin faces, the glass line, full-height mullions filled), 3D as two geometries of boxes (frame metal, panels glass or plaster), fields; plus dead DXF/export members (F-0047).
- src/elements/kinds/dimension.ts: Permanent dimensions in the registry: validation of anchors, derivation (placed or lost), held by their walls/openings (deleted with them), selection by line or value, moving (free ends only), Modify-tool behaviour (ends follow what goes, mirrored ends swapped, copies held by the copies), plan drawing with value, EQ and padlock, keep-out boxes for room tags, fields; plus a DXF writer left from the exports (F-0047).
- src/elements/kinds/floor.ts: Floors and ceilings in the registry: validation, derivation (from a room or an outline; stair wells cut out; layer materials), following a room (copied with it, released to its outline when the room goes), selection, moving/mapping/transforming, plan drawing (fill, tiling clipped inside, dashed or dotted outline, holes crossed), 3D slab or one slab per build-up layer, caps, fields (with an edge guardrail action); plus dead export members (F-0047).
- src/elements/kinds/grid.ts: Grid axes in the registry (on every level, not pasted): validation, derivation (the axis itself), selection (its line or its bubbles first), moving/mapping, a copy named on, plan drawing (dash-dot line, a bubble with the name past each end), fields; plus a dead DXF writer (F-0047).
- src/elements/kinds/guide.ts: Guides in the registry: endless lines (or points and circles) over the walls; validation (unit direction), selection within the guide tool's pixel reach, moving/mapping (direction and circle radius by the transform), plan drawing (a dashed endless line, a cross-in-circle point, a dashed circle with its centre), fields; dead export members (F-0047).
- src/elements/kinds/measure.ts: Measure marks in the registry: angle (arc between arms, degrees), an arc wall's radius / diameter / length (held by the wall), a point's coordinates; their text, lines, text place, validation, selection by text or lines, moving/mapping/transforming, plan drawing (ticks, arrows, centre mark, upright value on a backing), fields; plus a DXF writer and DXF text left from the exports (F-0047).
- src/elements/kinds/note.ts: Text notes, callouts (a note with a leader) and level marks in the registry: validation, derivation (a level mark's text is the height it says), selection by box or leader, moving/mapping (letters stay upright), plan drawing (lines centred, leader with arrowhead, level symbol), fields; plus a dead DXF writer (F-0047).
- src/elements/kinds/object.ts: Parametric objects in the registry: derivation cached per instance (definition evaluated into pieces, plan rings lowest first, voids, the turned box, floor/bottom/top), 3D geometry per piece (box, rounded box, cylinder, sphere/ellipsoid, revolve, profile, mesh) with voids cut out by CSG, pieces merged per material, crease edges for cut pieces, round-piece side counts by ARC_SAG, plan drawing (hidden at coarse detail), fields; plus dead GLB/DXF members (F-0047).

- src/elements/wall/join/cut-l.ts: Two wall ends meeting at a node (an L). - tests/tools/snap-lock.test.ts: Tests of inference locks (phase 9). A lock holds the next point to a direction from the last one: an arrow's axis, a wall's line or the line square to it, or Shift's direction. The point's length falls on the grid, and the lock works both ways along its line. It stops where its line crosses a wall or a guide near the pointer, before the grid; a crossing far from the pointer is ignored. With no point drawn from there is no lock. Separately, a point the pointer rested on aligns the next points across or up from it, and draws a dashed line.
